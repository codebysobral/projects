#The difference between <head> and <body>:

## What is <head>?

- The `<head>` tag is used to store page settings, such as formatting (`<meta charset="UTF-8" />`) and title (`<title>First Page</title>`).

## What is <body>?

- The <body> tag contains the page's content, such as heading tags, links, images, videos, and everything else that forms part of the page's content.

# What's the difference?

- The `<head>` tag stores page settings, and the `<body>` tag stores the page content.

- Example: In a restaurant, the tables are arranged in a certain way (`<head>` tag), and the food is the main attraction of the restaurant (`<body>` tag).
